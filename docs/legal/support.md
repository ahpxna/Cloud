## Common questions

**I can't sign in.** After five wrong passwords, sign-in for that email pauses
for ten minutes. Wait, then try again. If you forgot your password, ask the
operator to reset it; every device will be signed out and you sign in again
with the new password.

**I lost my phone.** On another signed-in device, open Uploads → Devices &
sessions and sign the lost phone out, or ask the operator to sign out every
device.

**I lost my authenticator app and recovery codes.** Contact the operator. After
confirming it is you, they can remove two-factor authentication so you can set
it up again.

**A photo is stuck in Uploads.** Open the app on Wi-Fi and keep it open until
the item shows *Available*. Uploads resume where they stopped. If an item keeps
failing, open Uploads → Diagnostics, review the log and export it to the
operator if you want help. Keep the photo in your Photos library until it shows
*Available* in the Library.

**The server says it is out of space.** Uploads pause safely; nothing already
backed up is affected. Tell the operator.

**I want a copy of my originals, or my account deleted.** Contact the operator.
See the privacy policy for how deletion and backups work.
